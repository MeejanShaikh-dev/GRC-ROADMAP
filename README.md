# GRC Roadmap

A structured roadmap for learning and building a practical **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)** cybersecurity portfolio.

This roadmap covers the journey from **cybersecurity fundamentals to advanced GRC practices**, including governance, risk management, compliance, auditing, ISO standards, NIST frameworks, cloud governance, privacy, business continuity, third-party risk, and executive reporting.

## ◈ Roadmap Coverage

* Cybersecurity Fundamentals
* Networking
* Operating Systems
* Cloud Fundamentals
* Governance
* Risk Management
* ISO/IEC 27001
* ISO/IEC 27002
* NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
* NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF)
* Security Policies & Standards
* Risk Registers & Risk Assessments
* Audit & Compliance
* Third-Party Risk Management
* Cloud Governance
* Privacy & Data Protection
* Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery
* Executive Security Reporting
* ISMS Documentation
* Security Architecture & Diagrams
* Security Awareness & Training
* GRC Templates
* Case Studies
* Interview Preparation

## ◈ Portfolio Goal

The goal is not only to learn GRC concepts, but to **build practical evidence of GRC skills** through documentation, risk assessments, policies, audit reports, compliance assessments, control mappings, dashboards, case studies, and hands-on projects.

The roadmap includes practical work such as hands-on labs, practical exercises, mini-projects, enterprise projects, and capstone projects.

## ◈ Core Frameworks & Standards

* ISO/IEC 27001
* ISO/IEC 27002
* NIST CSF
* NIST RMF
* ISO 31000
* COBIT
* CIS Controls
* ISO/IEC 27701
* ISO/IEC 22301
* ISO/IEC 42001

## ◈ Objective

Build a **job-ready GRC portfolio** that demonstrates practical understanding of cybersecurity governance, risk management, compliance, auditing, and security operations.

### → Learning Path

**Learn → Practice → Document → Build Projects → Create Portfolio → Prepare for Career**
